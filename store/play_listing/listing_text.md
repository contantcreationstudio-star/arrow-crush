## 3) Full description (max 4000)

Arrow Crush is the arrow puzzle game with a crunchy twist. Tap an arrow and it flies off the board. Hit an arrow of the same colour and both get CRUSHED! Line up same-colour arrows, set off chain reactions and watch one smart tap clear half the board.

If you love tap puzzle games, arrows escape puzzles and brain teasers, this is the arrow puzzle that does something new: arrows don't just leave the board, they smash into each other.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap an arrow: it flies off the way it points if nothing is in its path.
• Hit an arrow of the same colour and both are crushed.
• Bump into a different colour and you lose a heart.
• Reach the crush goal, then a school of flying fish carries off the arrows that are left.
• Keep all 3 hearts to win 3 stars.

500 ARROW PUZZLE LEVELS IN 25 WORLDS
Travel from Sunny Meadow and Bubble Bay through the wavy Snake Jungle, Coral Reef, Cookie Castle, Moon Base and many more, all the way to Rainbow Summit. Every level has its own board shape and no two boards are the same. Relaxing levels, hard levels and super hard levels take turns, so there is always a fresh logic puzzle to solve.

SPECIAL ARROWS & COMBOS
Clear several arrows with one tap to earn a special arrow:
• Drill smashes through every arrow in its path.
• Bomb blows up the area around it.
• Laser clears a whole row and column.
• Rainbow removes every arrow of one colour.
Fly two special arrows into each other for a mega combo!

NEW TWISTS EVERY FEW LEVELS
Pop balloons, break wooden crates, crack frozen ice, bounce arrows off mirrors, hit targets, fly golden arrows out, untangle linked arrows, time the flip arrows and chase the coin arrows. Each new piece gets a few levels of its own before it mixes in with the rest.

DAILY CHALLENGE
One new arrow maze every day, the same for everyone. Finish it for bonus coins, keep your daily streak alive and collect streak rewards.

STUCK? WE'VE GOT YOU
Undo a move, ask for a hint or use a booster. Hints and boosters come free with a short video whenever you choose to watch one, never forced. If a level is giving you trouble, the game quietly makes the next try a little kinder.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Offline puzzle game: no Wi-Fi or internet needed to play the levels.
• No account and no sign-up.
• Your progress is saved on your phone.
• Colourful graphics and satisfying crush effects.
• Short levels, perfect for a quick brain break.
• Free to play.

Tap, crush, chain! If you enjoy arrow games, tap away puzzles, colour match games, logic puzzles and brain games, Arrow Crush is made for you. Download it free and see how far you can go!

Arrow Crush is free to play and contains ads. Optional reward videos give extra hints, boosters and more.
