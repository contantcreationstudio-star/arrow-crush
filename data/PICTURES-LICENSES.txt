Arrow Crush - picture shapes (data/pictures.txt)

The level pictures are solid silhouettes made by tools/build_pictures.gd from these
open-licence icon sets (downloaded from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@iconify-json/<set>/icons.json):

- mdi               Material Design Icons, Pictogrammers      Apache License 2.0
- material-symbols  Material Symbols, Google                  Apache License 2.0
- ph                Phosphor Icons                            MIT License
- fa6-solid         Font Awesome Free 6, Fonticons, Inc.      CC BY 4.0 (https://fontawesome.com/license/free)
- icon-park-solid   IconPark, ByteDance                       Apache License 2.0
- fluent-emoji-flat Fluent Emoji, Microsoft                   MIT License

Each icon was rasterised, its inner details closed and filled (a solid outline), and
scaled down to a small bit mask. Credits are shown in the game under Options > Licences.
